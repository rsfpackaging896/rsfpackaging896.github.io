Packaging Guide: Types, Materials and Tips

Good packaging protects your product, shows your brand and helps customers trust you. This guide explains the basics in simple words.

Common Types of Packaging

  • Primary packaging: the layer that touches the product, such as a bottle or pouch.
  • Secondary packaging: the outer box that holds one or more primary packs.
  • Tertiary packaging: pallets and large cartons used for shipping.

Popular Packaging Materials

  • Cardboard and paperboard: light, low cost and easy to print on.
  • Corrugated board: strong and good for shipping boxes. You can see some packaging options here.
  • Plastic: water resistant and flexible, but check recycling options.
  • Kraft paper: natural look and eco friendly.

How to Choose the Right Packaging

  1. Know the size, weight and fragility of your product.
  2. Think about shipping distance and storage.
  3. Match the design with your brand colors and message.
  4. Compare cost, quality and minimum order quantity.
  5. Choose recyclable materials when possible.
